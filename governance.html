Governance

Mission continuity across distinct legal structures.

BioQCore separates mission-level principles from jurisdiction-specific legal authority. Current status, future structures and governance concepts are presented according to their actual stage of development.

Legal status

BioQCore is currently operating as a research and infrastructure initiative. A Delaware LLC is planned as a potential U.S. legal and operational vehicle but has not yet been formed. Additional jurisdiction-specific structures may be established in the future where they are functionally and legally appropriate.

One mission, distinct legal structures

Future entities or legal vehicles will not be treated as interchangeable. Each will have its own jurisdiction, authority, governing documents and defined role while supporting the same mission-level principles.

Governance model

Operational stewardship

Current work is coordinated at the initiative level. Formal management authority, member rights and fiduciary responsibilities will be defined by the governing documents of any entity that is formed.

Legal and operational roles

Jurisdiction-specific liaison, management and operational roles may be established where required. Their authority will derive from applicable law and executed governing documents, not from website descriptions.

Independent oversight

A multidisciplinary oversight model, including the Council of Seven concept, is under design for scientific, ethical and mission-level review. Its composition, authority and procedures are not yet finalized.

Future governance mechanisms

BioQCore does not currently operate a DAO, issue a token or conduct a token sale. Any future use of distributed governance or digital-asset mechanisms would require a demonstrated functional need and appropriate legal, tax, privacy and regulatory review before implementation.

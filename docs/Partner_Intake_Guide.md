# Partner Intake Guide

Document status: Public current guide
Channel status: MVP
Last reviewed: 2026-08-26

BioQCore provides a controlled public contact path for preliminary, non-confidential dialogue.

## Inquiry categories

- Research collaboration
- Grant / foundation inquiry
- Legal / compliance inquiry
- Investor / strategic partner inquiry
- Developer / open-source contribution
- Media inquiry

## Public-channel boundary

Do not submit patient-identifiable information, medical records, scans, DICOM or genomic data, private keys, confidential contracts, security-sensitive material or emergency requests through public forms or public email.

The current Partner form prepares an email in the user's local email client. BioQCore.org does not submit or store the form contents on the website itself.

Submission of an inquiry does not establish a partnership, contractual relationship, funding commitment or other formal relationship with BioQCore.

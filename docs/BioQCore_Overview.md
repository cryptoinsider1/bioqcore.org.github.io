# BioQCore Overview

Version: 1.0.0-rc
Document status: Public current overview
Last reviewed: 2026-08-26

BioQCore is a trust-first humanitarian and scientific research infrastructure initiative currently in a research/design/prototype phase.

## What BioQCore is

- A research and infrastructure initiative.
- A human-centric trust-oriented foundation for work across medicine, AI, cryptography and secure scientific collaboration.
- A public point of reference for researchers, prospective partners, compliance reviewers and future consortium participants.

## What BioQCore is not at this stage

- Not a hospital or medical provider.
- Not a clinical trial operator through this website.
- Not a provider of medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency support.
- Not an investment offer, token sale or financial advice platform.
- Not a patient-data intake portal.

## Status principle

BioQCore distinguishes current capabilities from concepts, planned components, prototypes and future mechanisms. A component or relationship should not be treated as operational, validated, partnered or legally established unless explicitly documented as such.

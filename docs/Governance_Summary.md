# Governance Summary

Document status: Public current summary
Maturity: Governance design / legal-structure preparation
Last reviewed: 2026-08-26

BioQCore governance architecture follows the model:

> mission continuity + distinct jurisdiction-specific legal structures

BioQCore is currently operating as a research and infrastructure initiative.

## Legal structures

A Delaware LLC is under consideration as a future U.S. legal and operational vehicle but has not yet been formed.

Additional jurisdiction-specific structures may be established separately where functionally and legally appropriate.

Future entities or legal vehicles are not interchangeable. Each must have its own jurisdiction, authority, governing documents and defined role while supporting shared mission-level principles.

## Governance concepts

The Council of Seven remains an ethical/scientific oversight concept under design unless and until formal governing documents establish its composition, authority and procedures.

No proposed governance body should be interpreted as possessing formal legal authority before that authority is established through the applicable governing documents and legal structure.
